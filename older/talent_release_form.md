### 1. Purpose of Data Collection
The Participant acknowledges and agrees that their photographic, optical, and volumetric facial data is being collected solely for:
1. Training, evaluating, and fine-tuning machine learning models for 3D facial mesh reconstruction, micro-displacement synthesis, and animation rigging.
2. Deriving synthetic, parameterized 3D facial geometry, ARKit blendshapes, and game-engine character assets.
3. Commercial game development, simulation software, interactive entertainment, and synthetic dataset generation.

### 2. Scope of Grant & Commercial License
The Participant hereby grants to the Data Controller and its successors, licensees, and assigns an irrevocable, perpetual, worldwide, transferable, sublicensable, royalty-free license to:

- Use derived 3D models and synthetic avatars in commercial and non-commercial video games, cinematics, software applications, and promotional materials.

### 3. Biometric Data & Privacy Notice
- **Notice of Biometric Processing:** In compliance with applicable biometric privacy regulations (including GDPR, CCPA, and Illinois BIPA), the Participant acknowledges that facial photographs and derived 3D coordinates constitute biometric data used to estimate geometric proportions.
- **Security & Storage:** Raw photographs and derived 3D assets are encrypted at rest and stored in secure, access-controlled repositories.
- **No Third-Party Reselling of Raw Biometrics:** Raw biometric source photographs will not be sold, leased, or traded to unrelated third parties for facial surveillance or personal identification purposes.
- **Synthetic Derivative Exemption:** Mathematically generalized neural network weights (e.g. MICA regressors, Detail GAN weights) and stylized in-game meshes derived through deformation transfer are considered synthetic technological derivatives.
